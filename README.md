🌍 ZAIVORA

Whole World. One Journey.

ZAIVORA is an AI-powered travel platform designed to help people plan smarter, understand trip costs, discover affordable destinations, and find better travel deals.

Built especially for students and budget travelers, ZAIVORA aims to make travel simple, transparent, and accessible worldwide.

✨ What ZAIVORA Does

* 🤖 AI Trip Cost Estimator — Get an estimated trip budget based on destination, duration, travelers, accommodation, food, transport, and activities.
* 💰 Budget-First Planning — Understand how much a trip may actually cost before making plans.
* 🌍 Affordable Destinations — Discover destinations that fit your budget.
* 🎓 Student-Friendly Travel — Designed with students and budget-conscious travelers in mind.
* 🏷️ Travel Deals — Find opportunities to save on travel.
* 🗺️ Smart Trip Planning — Bring important trip-planning decisions into one simple platform.

🎯 Our Vision

We believe traveling should not feel confusing or financially impossible.

ZAIVORA’s goal is to help users answer one important question:

“Can I afford this trip?”

Before booking anything, ZAIVORA helps users understand the expected cost and plan accordingly.
